### Chapter 1 - Burnwood gone
It all began on a night that should have 
been like any other. But that night became 
the end of everything. A horde of mysterious 
creatures suddenly invaded Burnwood, flames 
devoured houses, villagers fled in panic.

In the middle of disaster, the village's old 
mage - wounded and unable to stand - called
the man there (why is he called?), the 
warrios left. Only him.
He pressed half of a broken amulet into his
hand and whispered:
> **"Go to the neighboring kingdom. Ask for 
help before it is too late. The fate of
Burnwood now rests on you."**

### Chapter 2 - The suivivaling time 
Infested Forest (AFTER)
He ran. Alone and unable to fight back, he had
no choice but to hide, to run, to survive. 
The forest was not empty - it was watching 
him. 

Later, he finally reached the neighboring 
kingdom. But there was no kingdom. Only
ashes, only silence. The horde had already
passed here too.
